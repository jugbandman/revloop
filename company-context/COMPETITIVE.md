## Common Competitive Objections & Responses

### Objection: "We're already using [Competitor]"

**Response Framework:**
1. Acknowledge: "That makes sense - [Competitor] is a solid choice."
2. Probe: "What's working well? What challenges are you facing?"
3. Differentiate: "Here's where we might add value..."
4. Offer: "Would it be helpful to show you how we approach [specific area]?"

### Objection: "[Competitor] is cheaper"

**Response Framework:**
1. Acknowledge: "I understand cost is important."
2. Reframe: "Let's talk about total value, not just price."
3. Differentiate: "Here's what you get with us that [Competitor] doesn't offer..."
4. ROI: "The ROI typically looks like..."

**Key Points:**
- [Point 1]
- [Point 2]

### Objection: "[Competitor] has more features"

**Response Framework:**
1. Acknowledge: "They do have a lot of features."
2. Reframe: "But do you actually need all those features?"
3. Focus: "We focus on what matters most: [key value]"
4. Prove: "Here's how our focused approach delivers better results..."

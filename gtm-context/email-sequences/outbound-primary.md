# Primary Outbound Email Sequence Template

**5-touch sequence for cold outbound. Customize based on your ICP and target segment.**

## Sequence Overview

**Total Duration:** 16 days
**Touches:** 5 emails
**Spacing:** 3-4 days between touches
**Goal:** Get discovery call

---

## Touch 1: Initial Outreach (Day 1)

**Subject Line Options:**
- [Value prop] for [Company]
- Quick question about [pain point]
- [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out
- [Company] + [relevant insight]

**Email Body:**

Hi [First Name],

I noticed [Company] [specific observation - recent news, initiative, challenge, etc.]. That's [relevant comment].

I work with [similar companies/roles] who are [relevant challenge]. Many are [specific outcome they achieve].

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute call to see if we might be a fit? I can share how [similar company] [achieved relevant outcome].

Best,
[Your Name]

**Personalization Points:**
- [Company] - specific observation
- [First Name] - use their actual name
- [Relevant challenge] - based on ICP research
- [Similar company] - if you have a case study

---

## Touch 2: Add Value (Day 4)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Re: [Previous subject]
- Quick follow-up + [resource]
- [Resource] for [Company]
- Thought you'd find this useful

**Email Body:**

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note from earlier this week.

I put together a [resource type - guide, case study, framework] on [relevant topic] that might be useful for [Company] given [specific context].

[Link to resource or brief summary]

If it's helpful, I'd love to chat about how [similar companies] are applying this. Worth a quick call?

Best,
[Your Name]

**Personalization Points:**
- Reference first email
- Provide actual value (not just "check out our website")
- Make resource relevant to their specific situation

---

## Touch 3: Social Proof (Day 8)

**Subject Line Options:**
- How [Similar Company] [achieved outcome]
- [Outcome] for [Similar Company]
- Quick case study
- Re: [Previous subject]

**Email Body:**

Hi [First Name],

I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief.

[Similar Company] was facing [similar challenge] and recently [achieved outcome] using [your approach/solution].

[Brief 1-2 sentence case study]

If [Company] is dealing with [similar challenge], I'd be happy to share how they did it. Worth 15 minutes?

Best,
[Your Name]

**Personalization Points:**
- Use relevant case study
- Make it relatable to their situation
- Keep it brief

---

## Touch 4: Final Attempt (Day 12)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Last try - [Value prop]
- One more thing...
- Quick question
- [Different angle/value prop]

**Email Body:**

Hi [First Name],

I know you're swamped, so I'll make this super quick.

If [Company] is [relevant challenge], I can help you [specific outcome] - similar to what we did for [Similar Company].

[One sentence value prop]

If not a fit, no worries - just let me know and I'll stop reaching out. But if it is, worth a quick call?

Best,
[Your Name]

**Personalization Points:**
- Try a different angle/value prop
- Make it easy to say no (reduces friction)
- Keep it very brief

---

## Touch 5: Breakup Email (Day 16, Optional)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Closing the loop
- Last email (I promise)
- One final note

**Email Body:**

Hi [First Name],

I've reached out a few times about [value prop] for [Company], but I don't want to be a pest.

If this isn't a fit right now, no problem - I'll stop reaching out.

But if you'd like to explore [value prop] in the future, feel free to reach out anytime. I'm always happy to help.

Best of luck with [relevant company initiative]!

Best,
[Your Name]

**Personalization Points:**
- Be respectful and professional
- Leave door open for future
- Reference something specific about their company

---

## Personalization Checklist

For each prospect, customize:
- [ ] Company name and specific observation
- [ ] First name (not "Hi there")
- [ ] Relevant challenge based on ICP research
- [ ] Similar company/case study (if applicable)
- [ ] Why now trigger (if known)
- [ ] Industry/role-specific language

## Best Practices

**Timing:**
- Send Tuesday-Thursday
- 8-10am or 2-4pm (recipient's timezone)
- Space 3-4 days apart

**Tone:**
- Conversational, not salesy
- Value-focused
- Respectful of their time
- Easy to say no

**Tracking:**
- Track opens, clicks, replies
- A/B test subject lines
- Adjust based on what works

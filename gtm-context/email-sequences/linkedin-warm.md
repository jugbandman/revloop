# LinkedIn Message Flows Template

**Message flows for warm/known contacts on LinkedIn. Keep messages short and conversational.**

## Scenario 1: Warm Contact (You've Met Them)

**Context:** You've met at an event, had a conversation, exchanged cards, etc.

### Message 1: Reconnection

Hi [First Name],

Great meeting you at [Event/Place]! I enjoyed our conversation about [topic you discussed].

I mentioned [your value prop] - thought you might find it useful given [relevant context about their company/role].

Worth a quick call to explore?

[Your Name]

**Timing:** Send within 24-48 hours of meeting

---

### Message 2: Follow-up (If No Response, Day 5)

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note from [Event]. 

I put together a [resource] on [topic] that might be useful for [Company]. [Link or brief summary]

If helpful, happy to chat about how [similar companies] are applying this.

[Your Name]

---

### Message 3: Final Attempt (If No Response, Day 10)

Hi [First Name],

I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief.

If [Company] is [relevant challenge], I can help you [outcome] - similar to [Similar Company].

Worth 15 minutes? If not, no worries - just let me know.

[Your Name]

---

## General Best Practices

**Message Length:**
- Keep under 300 characters
- 2-3 sentences max
- Easy to read on mobile

**Tone:**
- Conversational, not salesy
- Personal, not generic
- Value-focused

**Timing:**
- Send connection request first (with note)
- Wait for acceptance before sending message
- Space follow-ups 3-5 days apart

**Personalization:**
- Always personalize opening
- Reference something specific (their post, company news, etc.)
- Use their first name

**Compliance:**
- Use LinkedIn's native messaging (not InMail unless necessary)
- Respect if they don't respond
- Don't spam

---

**Customize these flows based on how warm the connection is and what you know about them.**

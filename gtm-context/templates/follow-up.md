# Follow-Up Email Template

**Use this template for post-call follow-up emails.**

## Follow-Up Email Structure

**Subject Line Options:**
- Following up on our call
- Next steps from our conversation
- [Resource] you requested
- Quick follow-up

---

## Email Template

**Hi [First Name],**

**Thank You:**
Thanks for taking the time to chat today about [topic]. I really enjoyed our conversation about [specific topic discussed].

**Summary:**
As we discussed, [Company] is [situation/challenge] and you're looking to [goal]. I think we might be able to help with [specific area].

**Key Points:**
- [Key point 1 from call]
- [Key point 2 from call]
- [Key point 3 from call]

**Action Items:**
As discussed, here are the next steps:

**For You:**
- [Action item 1]
- [Action item 2]

**For Me:**
- [Action item 1: e.g., "I'll send you [resource] by [date]"]
- [Action item 2: e.g., "I'll schedule [next step] for [date]"]

**Next Steps:**
[Proposed next step: e.g., "Would you like to schedule a demo call? I'm available [times]."]

**Resources:**
As promised, here's [resource] we discussed: [link or attachment]

**Closing:**
Looking forward to [next step]. Let me know if you have any questions.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

## Variations

### If Qualified (Yes Path)

**Next Steps Section:**
"Based on our conversation, I think we'd be a great fit. Here's what I propose:

1. [Next step 1: e.g., "Schedule a demo with your team"]
2. [Next step 2: e.g., "Send you a proposal"]
3. [Timeline: e.g., "Start by [date]"]

Would you like to schedule [next step]? I'm available [times]."

---

### If Not Qualified (No Path)

**Next Steps Section:**
"I understand [Company] isn't ready to move forward right now because [reason]. No problem!

I'll check back in [timeframe] to see if things have changed. In the meantime, I'll send you [resource] that might be helpful.

If your situation changes, feel free to reach out anytime."

---

### If Need More Information

**Next Steps Section:**
"To better understand if we're a fit, I'd love to learn more about [specific area]. 

Could you help me understand:
- [Question 1]
- [Question 2]

Once I have this information, I can [next action]."

---

## Best Practices

**Timing:**
- Send within 24 hours of call
- Best times: Morning (8-10am) or afternoon (2-4pm)

**Tone:**
- Professional but friendly
- Reference specific points from call
- Make it easy to respond

**Length:**
- Keep it concise (under 200 words if possible)
- Use bullet points for action items
- Make next steps clear

---

**Customize this template based on the call outcome and next steps.**

# Discovery Call Outline Template

**Use this as a starting point for discovery call preparation. Customize based on your ICP and prospect.**

## Call Structure

### Opening (5 minutes)

**Rapport Building:**
- "Thanks for taking the time to chat today."
- "I'd love to learn more about [their role/company]."
- "How did you get into [their field]?" (if appropriate)

**Set Agenda:**
- "My goal today is to understand your challenges and see if we might be a fit."
- "I'll ask some questions about [topic areas], and then we can discuss if it makes sense to explore further."
- "Sound good?"

### Discovery (20-25 minutes)

**Role & Responsibilities:**
- "Tell me about your role - what are you responsible for?"
- "What does a typical day/week look like for you?"
- "What are your main priorities right now?"

**Current State:**
- "How are you currently handling [relevant area]?"
- "What tools/processes are you using today?"
- "What's working well? What's not?"

**Pain Points:**
- "What are your biggest challenges with [relevant area]?"
- "What's the impact of [challenge] on your team/company?"
- "What have you tried before to solve this?"
- "Why hasn't that worked?"

**Qualification:**
- "What would solving [pain point] mean for you?"
- "How urgent is this? What happens if you don't solve it?"
- "Who else is involved in decisions like this?"
- "What's your timeline for addressing this?"

**Decision Process:**
- "How do you typically evaluate new solutions?"
- "What factors matter most in your decision?"
- "Who else would need to be involved?"
- "What's your typical timeline for decisions like this?"

### Qualification Check (5 minutes)

**Confirm Understanding:**
- "So if I understand correctly, you're facing [challenge] and it's impacting [impact], and you're looking to [goal] by [timeline]. Is that right?"

**Check Fit:**
- "Based on what you've shared, I think we might be able to help with [specific area]. Would it make sense to show you how we approach this?"

**Next Steps:**
- "What would be most helpful next? A demo? A proposal? More information?"

### Closing (5 minutes)

**Summarize:**
- "Let me make sure I captured everything correctly..."
- [Summarize key points]

**Confirm Next Steps:**
- "So our next step would be [specific action] - does that work for you?"
- "When would be a good time to [next step]?"

**Set Expectations:**
- "I'll send you [deliverable] by [timeframe]."
- "I'll follow up on [date] to [action]."

---

## Key Principles

**Listen More Than You Talk:**
- Aim for 80/20 (they talk 80%, you talk 20%)
- Ask open-ended questions
- Let them tell their story

**Confirm Understanding:**
- "So if I understand correctly..."
- "Let me make sure I got this right..."
- "What I'm hearing is..."

**Don't Pitch Too Early:**
- Discovery first, then determine if there's a fit
- If you pitch too early, you miss learning about their actual needs

**Take Notes:**
- Pain points mentioned
- Decision process details
- Timeline and urgency
- Budget indicators
- Other stakeholders
